Does perfection really exist?（完美真的存在吗？）
- Visual Perfection（视觉上的完美）：直线、平行线、几何图形。焦距
- Computational Perfection（计算中的完美）：数据传输的准确性、无偏差。


I'm thinking about **Perfection** because I feel that perfect things may not really exist.
From different viewpoints, we can see different sides of the same thing.
So I want to use different viewpoints to **challenge the idea of one single perfection.**
