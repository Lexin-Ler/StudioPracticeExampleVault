
**Keywords:** Visual Experience · Physical Space · Interaction

### 1. Summary

I am more interested in making art in physical spaces. For this project, I want to explore visual experience through an interactive installation.

The work may be a box with miniature scenes inside. Viewers can look into the box from different viewpoints and see different scenes. I also want to use sensors and programming so that viewers can affect the scenes while looking at the work.

### 2. Concept

I want to create scenes that are different from our everyday experiences. By looking at the same space from different viewpoints, viewers may have a new visual experience. They may also see familiar objects in a new way.

I am still exploring the content and narrative of the scenes. For the final exhibition, I hope the different scenes and their changes can be connected or explore the same topic.

### 3. Format & Technical Considerations & Equipment

#### Installation Overview

For this WIP exhibition, I plan to show an experimental version of the work, rather than a finished artwork. I want to make an interactive installation using sensors and handmade elements.

The installation may be a box with miniature scenes inside. The base will be about 40 × 40 cm, and the height is not decided yet. Viewers can look inside from different viewpoints.

I am considering using ultrasonic sensors or similar devices to detect the viewer's position or distance when they look into the box. This information will be used to change the scenes inside. For example, I may add small screens or lights inside the box. Through Physical Computing and programming, the viewer's movements or distance may change the lights, images or other electronic elements in the scenes.

For materials, I am considering using coloured acetate sheets, textiles and other materials to make the scenes inside the box.

#### Why I Want to Include Interaction

I do not want the installation to be just a static display inside a box. Although the scenes are inside a small physical space, the movements and actions of viewers outside the box can also change what happens inside.

In this way, I hope to connect the space inside the box with the space outside it. This is an important reason why I want to use interaction and Physical Computing.

#### Exhibition Setup

I hope to place the installation in a corner of the exhibition space, with walls around it. There should be enough space for viewers to come closer or move around the box. This will also help the sensors detect their positions.

### 4. Context

#### Samuel van Hoogstraten — _A Peepshow with Views of the Interior of a Dutch House_

I saw this perspective box at the National Gallery. This work inspired me in its use of physical space, perspective, form and visual experience.

I find it very clever that the artist uses a physical box to show two-dimensional images. The work feels like a small theatre stage. At the same time, it uses perspective to create the illusion of a three-dimensional space.

#### Olafur Eliasson — _Room for one colour_

This work uses monochromatic lighting to limit how people normally see colours. At the same time, it gives viewers a new way to experience the space around them.

I am interested in how changing the conditions of vision can make people experience the same physical space differently.

#### Julio Le Parc

Julio Le Parc has made many interactive works that are similar to playground equipment. He uses simple physical principles and carefully designed mechanical structures. Viewers can experience different visual effects by moving and taking part in the work.

I am especially interested in how he uses light, reflection, movement and repeated structures to create rhythm and a theatrical atmosphere in physical spaces.

His work shows me that complex visual experiences do not always need complex technology. Simple mechanical movements and physical structures can also create rich visual effects.

This has inspired my installation. I want to combine physical materials, spatial structures and viewer interaction, so that the same scene can look different depending on where the viewer stands and how they interact with it.

### 5. Plan

|Visual Terms|Current Direction|

Next, I want to explore more visual methods that may work for my project. I plan to look at theatrical stage design, readymade art, deconstruction and other playful visual art practices. I hope to find more ideas about how to build scenes and show everyday objects in different ways.

I will also continue to study how Physical Computing can work with physical materials and spatial structures.

For the making process, I plan to build a basic box first. I will test different materials and structures, including perspective techniques, translucent materials and different ways of building spaces. I want to see what visual effects they can create.

After that, I will add simple interactive elements step by step. I will test sensors and programming, and try to connect the visual structure with the interactive system.

Finally, based on the experiments and further research into the narrative, I hope to decide what everyday objects to include in the scenes, why they change and how they change. I will use these results to develop the content and concept of the work, and prepare an experimental installation that viewers can interact with at the WIP exhibition.
